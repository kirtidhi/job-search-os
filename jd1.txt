Product Manager, Platform Capabilities
Join Cartology, the team that's driving real customer impact in the moments that matter most

Surry Hills (Sydney) based with 2 days WFH
We are Cartology
Cartology is one of Australia’s leading retail media businesses, powered by the Woolworths Group. We provide targeted omnichannel marketing solutions and comprehensive closed loop reporting to help brands grow. With Cartology, brands can drive real customer impact across the shopper journey.
Cartology is truly pioneering the future of Retail Media, creating, shaping and leading this new industry that connects brands to customers in new and exciting ways.
Be nothing but yourself in a culture that is inclusive and innovative. Work as part of an ambitious, smart and passionate team and belong to something meaningful as part of the Woolworths Group.
What you'll do
The Product Manager, Platform Capabilities is a critical role in the Digital Experiences team within the Tech & Data business unit. You will oversee and advocate for product and platform capability and systems health, delivering features and new integrations, and facilitating a positive developer experience for the wider engineering team.
Key responsibilities of the position include:
Product Leadership Collaborate with tech, design and broader stakeholders to define and deliver a strategy and roadmap that enables our platform, business and client objectives.
Platform Strategy: Lead the strategic direction of complex, back-end driven platform capabilities, system architectures, and user journey / experience flows that power the Ads Manager platform.
Scalability & Reusability: Define foundational systems, rules, and frameworks that can be scaled and re-used across multiple teams and programs within the Ads Manager platform.
Client-Centric Translation: Translate complex backend requirements and capabilities into intuitive, scalable client experiences and use cases, partnering closely with other experience owners to deliver.
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with engineers, designers, and other product owners to align on system capabilities, constraints, and the most elegant ways to surface technical functionality to the user
Operational Excellence: Champion product best practices, experimentation, and ensure the voice of the Client is represented in the products and platforms you manage.
What you’ll bring
Proven solid experience in product management roles, demonstrating expertise in simplifying complex technical strategies for diverse audiences.
Domain knowledge in retail media, eCommerce platforms, complex SaaS platforms, enterprise software, or internal tooling.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and data-driven decision-making abilities, combined with exceptional communication skills to influence technical and non-technical stakeholders.
High attention to detail, consistently maintaining quality standards while ensuring a client-centric perspective in all work and decision-making processes.
Demonstrate accountability and a proactive attitude, fostering excellent collaboration within team environments and maintaining a growth mindset that incorporates feedback.
What you'll experience
A commitment to flexibility through a hybrid working model which combines time spent at a support office or hub and time working from home, supporting both in-person connection and balancing the flexibility that remote working provides for our teams.
High-impact role supporting our teams who enrich our communities.
A global business with endless career possibilities around every corner and across every discipline - with valuable exposure to a vast and exciting business network.
A range of programs to help you prioritise and manage your wellbeing, including 24/7 access to the Sonder app.
A progressive and competitive leave policy that gives you more space for what matters to you.
Team discounts across our range of Woolworths Group brands you know and love and a robust rewards program that celebrates and incentivises purpose-driven work.
Endless possibilities with Woolworths Group
We’re a proud part of the Woolworths Group – 200,000+ bright minds, passionate hearts and unique perspectives working together connected by a shared Purpose – ‘to create better experiences together for a better tomorrow.’
Here you’ll be supported and empowered to explore new ideas, make brave commitments and innovate better ways to meet the food and everyday needs of more than 24 million customers every week. Backed by a culture of genuine care, a flexible approach to work and the support to grow your career and make a meaningful impact, the possibilities for what we can achieve together are endless.
